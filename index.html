Gurat, located on india's western coast, is a land of vibrant culture, rich histrory, and breataking diversity. From the ancient ruins of Sholavira to the majestic Asiatic lions of Gir, and the colorful festivals that electrify its streets, Gujarat offers an Unforgettable experiene.
